What the results reveal · analysis audit

Four winners were missed: Classy Touch (R1), Secret Saint (R4), Bashir (R5) and Her Ladyship (R7). Three winners were covered by our selections: EK (R2), Espionage (R3) and Stars Envied (R6). Equine Power, our Day’s Best, missed the top three, while Next Best Espionage won.

RACE 1 · CLASSY TOUCH

Classy Touch won the Donegal Plate; Rajendra was 3rd, but Zorba and American Affair missed the top three. Audit: revisit the full-field 1600m fitness/staying comparison, declared weight and the evidence supporting Zorba as top choice.

RACE 4 · SECRET SAINT

Secret Saint won while our Main Danger Annhilator finished 2nd. Audit: check whether class/ratings and recent-form comparisons gave sufficient credit to Secret Saint. Our danger assessment identified a strong runner, but the winner was missed.

RACE 5 · BASHIR

Bashir won and our 1st Choice Decoy finished 2nd. Audit: examine comparative weight, class, current form and trainer/jockey evidence for Bashir against Decoy. The selection found the runner-up but not the winner.

RACE 7 · HER LADYSHIP

Her Ladyship won; Modin and Queen Eternal finished 2nd and 3rd as our 2nd Choice and Main Danger. Audit: review the top end of the field and how we weighted recent form, draw and effective weight for the winner.

RACES 2, 3 & 6 · WINNERS COVERED

EK and Espionage won as our 1st Choices. Stars Envied won as our 2nd Choice. This shows why we separately report first-choice wins and winners covered by the full three-horse selection set.

Changes to apply in future studies

  • Rank the entire field before finalising the three selections; do not assess only the leading few.
  • Keep official handicap rating, declared weight and effective weight as separate fields.
  • Assess draw and likely pace in the context of distance and track configuration.
  • Compare class and quality of recent opposition before rewarding a low weight.
  • For 3-year-old maidens, assess every runner's development, pedigree and available race/work evidence; mark gaps clearly.
  • Trackwork supports fitness assessment but must never be substituted for race-speed evidence.
  • Record missing information as unknown, not as a negative score.

This is a qualitative post-race audit based on the published selections and official top-three finishers. It does not claim to establish the precise cause of each result or recalibrate the scoring formula from one meeting.

Transparency & methodology

We preserve each pre-race selection exactly as published. A winner identified as 2nd Choice or Main Danger is recorded as a covered winner, not as a first-choice success. Main Danger is counted as a published selection for top-three coverage. Additional dangers are not included in the three-selection score. Unverified finishing positions are not guessed.

Two additional winners identified only by the Reserve: Sanaya (R2) and Santana Row (R7). Renaissance (R4) was not included in any of the four published positions; Fortis, our Reserve, finished 2nd.

Changes to apply in future studies

  • Rank the entire field before finalising selections.
  • Keep official handicap rating, declared weight and effective weight separate.
  • Assess draw and likely pace against distance and track configuration.
  • Compare class and quality of recent opposition before rewarding a low weight.
  • For 3-year-olds, assess development, pedigree and available race/work evidence; mark gaps clearly.
  • Use trackwork to assess fitness, not as a substitute for verified race-speed evidence.
  • Record missing information as unknown, not as a negative score.

This is a qualitative post-race audit; it does not claim to establish the precise cause of each result.

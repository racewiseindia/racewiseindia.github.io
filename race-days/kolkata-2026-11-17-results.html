What the results reveal · analysis audit

Four winners covered by our three tracked selections: Mrs Brando (R1), Saiko (R3), Luther (R5) and Shine (R6). Three were first-choice winners; Mrs Brando was our 2nd Choice.

Two additional winners identified only by the Reserve: Sanaya (R2) and Santana Row (R7). Renaissance (R4) was not included in any of the four published positions; Fortis, our Reserve, finished 2nd.

RACE 2 · SANAYA

Sanaya won from our Reserve position while all three tracked selections missed the top three. Audit: revisit the full-field ranking and the evidence that placed Sanaya below the leading three; distinguish the reserve's positive signals from the final ordering.

RACE 4 · RENAISSANCE

Renaissance won while Queen Anula, our 1st Choice, finished 3rd and Fortis, our Reserve, finished 2nd. Audit: review why the winner was absent from all four positions and whether the class, weight and recent-form comparison adequately ranked the entire field.

RACE 7 · SANTANA ROW

Santana Row won from our Reserve position; Tetsu, our 1st Choice and Day’s Best, finished 2nd. Audit: review the relative assessment of Santana Row and Tetsu, without treating the runner-up result as a win.

RACE 6 · STRONGEST TRACKED COVERAGE

Shine, Abhicandra and Victoria Cross filled the official top three in the same order as 1st Choice, Main Danger and 2nd Choice. This race delivered full top-three coverage from the tracked selections.

Changes to apply in future studies

  • Rank the entire field before finalising the selections; do not assess only the leading few.
  • Keep official handicap rating, declared weight and effective weight as separate fields.
  • Assess draw and likely pace in the context of distance and track configuration.
  • Compare class and quality of recent opposition before rewarding a low weight.
  • For 3-year-old maidens, assess development, pedigree and available race/work evidence; mark gaps clearly.
  • Trackwork supports fitness assessment but must never be substituted for verified race-speed evidence.
  • Record missing information as unknown, not as a negative score.

This is a qualitative post-race audit. It does not claim to establish the precise cause of each result or recalibrate the scoring formula from one meeting.

Transparency & methodology

Original pre-race selections are preserved exactly as published. The performance score tracks 1st Choice, 2nd Choice and Main Danger only. Reserve results are displayed separately and do not alter the three-selection totals. A winner in 2nd Choice or Main Danger is a covered winner, not a first-choice win. Unverified information is not presented as fact.

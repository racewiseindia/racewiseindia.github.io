Race-by-Race Comparison

RACE 1 · THE AZHAR HANDICAP · 1100M

RaceWise order: Rosalia (1) · Star Pride (2) · Ananta Sukha (3) · Trail Blazer (4). Reserve: Gold Script.

Official top four: 1 Star Pride · 2 Rosalia · 3 Diamond Gold · 4 Gold Script.

Review: Star Pride won as our second-ranked selection; Rosalia finished second. Gold Script, our reserve, finished fourth. Two ranked selections in the top three; reserve separately covered fourth.

RACE 2 · THE MING DYNASTY HANDICAP · 1100M

RaceWise order: Soleva (1) · City Of Joy (2) · Ashwa Brazil (3) · Kay Star (4). Reserve: Aspira.

Official top four: 1 City Of Joy · 2 Soleva · 3 Ashwa Brazil · 4 Kay Star.

Review: All four ranked selections filled the first four. City Of Joy won as our second choice; Soleva, our Day’s Best, finished second. This was a strong full-field order coverage, although our top choice did not win.

RACE 3 · THE VOLUNTARY HANDICAP · 1400M

RaceWise order: Romping Home (1) · Exceed (2) · Trillionaire (3) · Sicyon (4). Reserve: Fable.

Official top four: 1 Sicyon · 2 Fable · 3 Romping Home · 4 Exceed.

Review: Three of our four ranked selections finished in the official top four. Sicyon won from our fourth-ranked position; Romping Home was third and Exceed fourth. Reserve Fable finished second and should be noted separately.

RACE 4 · THE MAYFOWL CUP · 1400M

RaceWise order: Trakila (1) · Mindful (2) · Blackstone (3). No reserve in the three-horse field.

Official result: 1 Trakila · 2 Blackstone · 3 Mindful.

Review: All three runners were covered. Trakila won as our first selection and 2nd Day’s Best. Blackstone and Mindful finished second and third respectively; our second and third order was reversed.

RACE 5 · THE ROBIN HOOD HANDICAP DIVISION I · 1400M

RaceWise order: Rambha (1) · Priceless Prince (2) · Vir aaj (3) · True Faith (4). Reserve: Memphis.

Official top four: 1 True Faith · 2 Rambha · 3 Lighting Spear · 4 Sabar.

Review: Rambha finished second and True Faith won from our fourth-ranked position. Priceless Prince finished eighth and Vir aaj fifth. Lighting Spear’s third place was missed by our ranked selections. Two of four ranked selections in the official top four.

RACE 6 · THE ROBIN HOOD HANDICAP DIVISION II · 1400M

RaceWise order: Hermanus (1) · Simran’s Attitude (2) · Spectra (3) · Saseka (4). Reserve: Don’tstopthemusic.

Official top four: 1 Saseka · 2 Spectra · 3 Marcolini · 4 Simran’s Attitude.

Review: Three of our four ranked selections finished in the official top four. Saseka won from our fourth-ranked position; Spectra finished second and Simran’s Attitude fourth. Hermanus finished sixth. The winner was identified, but ranked too low.

Day’s Best — Final Review

DAY’S BEST · SOLEVA · RACE 2

FINISHED 2ND

Soleva ran second behind City Of Joy. The selection reached the first two, but did not win.

2ND DAY’S BEST · TRAKILA · RACE 4

WON · 1ST

Trakila won the Mayfowl Cup, validating the second Day’s Best selection.

What We Learned — Analysis Audit

  • Winner identification: all six winners appeared within our ranked top four. However, only Trakila was ranked first and won.
  • Ranking accuracy: Sicyon, True Faith and Saseka won from fourth-ranked positions. These are clear cases where our analysis identified the winner but ranked it too low.
  • Good field coverage: all four Race 2 selections finished in the top four; all three Race 4 runners were covered.
  • Missed placing: Lighting Spear’s third in Race 5 was not included in our ranked four.
  • Reserve note: Gold Script (R1) and Fable (R3) finished fourth and second respectively. These reserve results are not included in the ranked-selection percentages.
  • Day’s Best: Soleva finished second and Trakila won. Both Day’s Best selections finished in the top two.

The review measures selection accuracy only. It does not imply profit, return on stake or guaranteed future performance.

What the results reveal · analysis audit

Four winners covered by our three tracked selections: Ragnarok (R2), You Tuber (R4), Shoolin (R7) and Advait (R8). Two were first-choice winners and two were second-choice winners. Timeless Elegance (R3) was identified only in the Reserve position and is reported separately.

Winners missed by the three tracked selections: Atreya (R1), Timeless Elegance (R3, reserve identified), Dino (R5) and My Touch? No — My Touch was covered as 2nd Choice in R6. The three-selection misses were Atreya and Dino; R3's winner was reserve-covered.

RACE 1 · ATREYA

Atreya won while our 1st Choice Char Ek Char finished 2nd. Audit: review the full-field comparison and the weight/class assessment that separated the winner from our leading selection.

RACE 3 · TIMELESS ELEGANCE

Timeless Elegance won from our Reserve position; Barbarossa, our Main Danger, finished 2nd. Audit: examine why the reserve outranked neither of the two leading choices, and whether the final ordering reflected the available evidence.

RACE 5 · DINO

Dino won and none of our three tracked selections reached the top three. Aashritha Pet, our Reserve, finished 2nd. Audit: revisit full-field class, weight, current form and trainer/jockey comparisons; do not infer a single cause from the result alone.

RACES 2, 4, 6, 7 & 8 · STRONG COVERAGE

Ragnarok, You Tuber, My Touch, Shoolin and Advait were all identified within the three tracked selections. R2, R4, R6, R7 and R8 each produced at least one top-three tracked selection, with the winners covered in all five.

Changes to apply in future studies

  • Rank the entire field before finalising the selections; do not assess only the leading few.
  • Keep official handicap rating, declared weight and effective weight as separate fields.
  • Assess draw and likely pace in the context of distance and track configuration.
  • Compare class and quality of recent opposition before rewarding a low weight.
  • For 3-year-old maidens, assess development, pedigree and available race/work evidence; mark gaps clearly.
  • Trackwork supports fitness assessment but must never be substituted for verified race-speed evidence.
  • Record missing information as unknown, not as a negative score.

This is a qualitative post-race audit. It does not claim to establish the precise cause of each result or recalibrate the scoring formula from one meeting.

Transparency & methodology

Original pre-race selections are preserved exactly as published. The performance score tracks 1st Choice, 2nd Choice and Main Danger only. Reserve results are displayed separately and do not alter the three-selection totals. A winner in 2nd Choice or Main Danger is a covered winner, not a first-choice win. Unverified information is not presented as fact.

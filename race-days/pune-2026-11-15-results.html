What the results reveal · analysis audit

Three winners were missed: Rules (R5), Styx (R7) and Rainbow Connection (R8). Five winners were covered by our three selections: Vortex (R1), Zephyr (R2), Queen’s Covenant (R3), Golden Eye (R4) and Fynbos (R6). Two of those covered winners were 2nd Choices, not 1st Choices. This distinction is central to our performance tracking.

RACE 5 · RULES

Rules won the Division I 1200m handicap and was not included in our three tracked selections. Absolute Emperor, our Main Danger, finished 2nd. Audit: recheck the full-field class/handicap comparison and whether improving or lower-weight runners received sufficient consideration. Do not infer a single cause from the result alone.

RACE 7 · STYX

Styx won the 3-year-old maiden. Our Main Danger Chilton Lodge finished 2nd and 2nd Choice Sabalenka finished 3rd, but our 1st Choice Lumineer missed the frame. Audit: revisit maiden-race development, pedigree and sibling evidence, and the reliability of limited race samples for lightly raced 3-year-olds.

RACE 8 · RAINBOW CONNECTION

Rainbow Connection won the Whopper Plate while all three selections missed the top three. Audit: review the full-field assessment, distance suitability, current form and weight/class balance. This is the clearest full-selection miss of the meeting.

RACES 1, 2, 3, 4 & 6 · WINNERS COVERED

Vortex, Zephyr, Queen’s Covenant, Golden Eye and Fynbos were all covered by our three selections. Vortex, Golden Eye and Fynbos were 1st Choices; Zephyr and Queen’s Covenant were 2nd Choices. Preserve this distinction in future performance tracking.

Changes to apply in future studies

  • Rank the entire field before finalising the three selections; do not assess only the leading few.
  • Keep official handicap rating, declared weight and effective weight as separate fields.
  • Assess draw and likely pace in the context of distance and track configuration.
  • Compare class and quality of recent opposition before rewarding a low weight.
  • For 3-year-old maidens, assess every runner's development, pedigree and available race/work evidence; mark gaps clearly.
  • Trackwork supports fitness assessment but must never be substituted for race-speed evidence.
  • Record missing information as unknown, not as a negative score.

This is a qualitative post-race audit based on the published selections and official top-three finishers. It does not claim to establish the precise cause of each result or recalibrate the scoring formula from one meeting.

Transparency & methodology

We preserve each pre-race selection exactly as published. A winner identified as 2nd Choice or Main Danger is recorded as a covered winner, not as a first-choice success. Main Danger is counted as a published selection for top-three coverage. Additional dangers are not included in the three-selection score. Unverified finishing positions are not guessed.

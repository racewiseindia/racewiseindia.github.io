What the results reveal · analysis audit

Seven winners appeared in our ranked top four: Routing Run (R1, 1st), Knotty Spirit (R3, 3rd), Zobel (R4, 4th), Knotty Emperor (R5, 2nd), Money Bags (R6, 1st), Irish Coffee (R7, 3rd) and Astraeus (R8, 1st).

One winner was identified only as Reserve: Ebotse won Race 2. This must not be counted as a top-four hit.

RACE 2 · EBOTSE — FIELD-RANKING MISS

Our reserve won, while the ranked top four contained only Makoto in 4th. Review the full-field order and the evidence that led Ebotse to be placed below the leading four.

RACE 4 · ZOBEL — RANKING TOO LOW

Zobel won from our 4th position, while reserve Darrington finished 3rd. Spark of Life was our top choice but finished 5th. Review the relative ranking and the weight/class/distance assessment.

RACE 5 · TEAK — TOP-RANKING ERROR

Teak finished 5th; Knotty Emperor, our 2nd Choice, won. Pledge and Final Call also ran into the top four. Review the ordering of the 1600m contenders.

RACE 7 · WINNER INCLUDED, RANKED THIRD

Irish Coffee won from our 3rd position; Jersey King finished 2nd. The winner was covered, but the order of the top two was reversed.

RACE 1 & RACE 3 · STRONG MULTI-RUNNER COVERAGE

Race 1 placed our first three ranked runners in the official top three. Race 3 also placed our first three ranked runners in the top three, although the winner was our 3rd Choice.

Changes to apply in future studies

  • Complete a full-field ranking audit before locking the top four and reserve, especially where the reserve is a live contender.
  • Recheck the relative order of runners where class, handicap burden and distance suitability point in different directions.
  • For 1100m and 1200m races, test early speed, draw and likely pace together rather than in isolation.
  • For 1600m races, give stamina and proven performance at the trip proper weight alongside current form and handicap.
  • Keep trackwork evidence separate from race-speed evidence; do not infer missing sectionals or timings.
  • Record withdrawals and DNC accurately and do not score them as ordinary finishing positions.

This is a qualitative post-race audit. It identifies where rankings missed or succeeded; it does not claim to prove the precise cause of each result.

Transparency & methodology

Final pre-race selections remain unchanged. The four ranked positions are measured as a group; the reserve is shown separately. A reserve winner is not counted as a top-four winner. First-choice wins, winner coverage, top-three finishes and Day’s Best are reported as separate measures. Figures are not betting returns.

Study integrity & final checks

All seven races have been studied across the accepted field, handicap weights and declared claims, recent form, class and distance suitability, available trackwork, sire/dam pedigree, equipment and declared connections. Verified race timings were available only for some runners; a complete comparable speed/sectional rating, full sibling and half-sibling records, complete trainer/jockey combination statistics and current veterinary reports were not available for every runner. These gaps have not been filled with estimates. No selection is a guaranteed outcome.

Before race time, recheck final declarations, late withdrawals, jockey changes, equipment changes and going updates. Race 7's official acceptance spelling for runner 12 differs from other racecard sources (Arjel/Aurele); verify the final declaration.

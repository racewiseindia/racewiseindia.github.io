What the results reveal · analysis audit

Four winners were missed: The Golden Dream (R1), Royal Vanguard (R4), Splendid Dream (R5) and Scarlette Lady (R6). These are the races to investigate first. A result alone does not prove which pre-race factor was misread.

RACE 1 · THE GOLDEN DREAM

Carter still placed 3rd, but The Golden Dream won from draw 2 carrying 60.5kg. Carter carried 62kg before the 3.5kg claim and started from draw 10. Audit: check how we weighted draw, effective weight, low-rated opposition and the winner's recent form. Do not assume the claim neutralised the wide draw.

RACE 4 · ROYAL VANGUARD

Knightsbridge finished 2nd and Conquistador 3rd, while Royal Vanguard won by 5½ lengths. Audit: strengthen the full-field review of 3-year-old maiden readiness, pedigree/development, recent race evidence and finishing ability. We identified two place-getters but missed the winner.

RACE 5 · SPLENDID DREAM

Carat Love finished 2nd, but Splendid Dream won; Flight Of Fancy was 5th and Flaming Sword 8th. Audit: compare class/rating strength and current performance more rigorously against weight and apprentice claims. Low effective weight must not automatically outweigh stronger class evidence.

RACE 6 · SCARLETTE LADY

Star Honour finished 2nd and Joyful Spirit 3rd, while Scarlette Lady won. Audit: review proven class, recent fitness, distance form and the weight carried by the winner. Effective weight alone did not identify the winner.

RACES 2, 3 & 7 · WINNERS COVERED

Eminent Gold won as our 2nd Choice; Born To Rise and Ravishing Queen won as our 1st Choices. Preserve these as successful examples, but check whether the same factors can be independently verified in future races.

Changes to apply in future studies

  • Rank the entire field before finalising the three selections; do not assess only the leading few.
  • Keep official handicap rating, declared weight and effective weight as separate fields.
  • Assess draw and likely pace in the context of distance and track configuration.
  • Compare class and quality of recent opposition before rewarding a low weight.
  • For 3-year-old maidens, assess every runner's development, pedigree and available race/work evidence; mark gaps clearly.
  • Trackwork supports fitness assessment but must never be substituted for race-speed evidence.
  • Record missing information as unknown, not as a negative score.

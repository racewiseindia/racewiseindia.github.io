RaceWise learning notes

  • Race 1: Carbon Warrior won from our second position after we restored Aeronautic to first. The later work comparison and draw advantage did not justify demoting Aeronautic; the result should prompt a review of how we weight early juvenile work, draw and limited race evidence.
  • Race 2: all four ranked selections filled the top four, but Casspian won from fourth. Revisit the ranking of the top-weighted runner and how the declared 5kg claim affected the effective weights of the other contenders.
  • Race 3: Lion’s Gate was missed entirely by the ranked four. This is the clearest coverage failure; audit the complete runner comparison, including its rating, effective weight, draw and previous form.
  • Race 4: all four ranked selections finished in the top four and Day’s Best Daring Spirit won. This was the strongest race-level result.
  • Race 5: all four ranked selections covered the official top four, but N R I Sunpower won from fourth. Review the ordering in this low-confidence race.
  • Race 6: Rival won as Main Danger and Normui finished second as fourth choice. The winner was identified in the main danger position, but not ranked first or second.

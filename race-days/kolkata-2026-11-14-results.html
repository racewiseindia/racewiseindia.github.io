What the results reveal · analysis audit

Only one winner was outside our three selections: Star Pride in Race 1. We covered the winners in Races 2–8, although four of those winners were ranked 2nd Choice or Main Danger rather than 1st Choice.

RACE 1 · STAR PRIDE

Hermes and Diamond Gold both placed, but Star Pride won. Audit the full-field comparison, recent sprint form, effective weight and draw rather than narrowing the analysis too early.

RACES 3, 4, 6 & 7 · RANKING ORDER

Magnifico, Voula, City Of Joy and Big Bay all won from our 2nd Choice or Main Danger positions. Revisit the evidence that separated these horses from our first choices. The aim is better ranking, not hindsight-based changes to the original record.

RACES 2, 5 & 8 · FIRST-CHOICE SUCCESSES

Dreamflight, Peaberry and Live It Love It won as our 1st Choices. Preserve these as positive examples and identify which pre-race indicators were strongest and independently verifiable.

Improvements for future meetings

  • Rank the full field before finalising the three selections.
  • Recheck the gap between 1st Choice and the other two selections using verified evidence.
  • Keep handicap rating, declared weight, apprentice allowance and effective weight distinct.
  • Evaluate draw and likely pace in relation to distance and track configuration.
  • Keep trackwork separate from race-speed evidence; never invent sectional or individual timings.
  • Record unavailable information as unknown, not as a negative.

This is a qualitative post-race audit, not proof of the precise cause of each result or a recalibration of the scoring formula from one meeting.
